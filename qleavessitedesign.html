About

The nursery behind the numbers

Undergrowth started as four shelves in a garage and a spreadsheet nobody asked for. The spreadsheet became the counter you scrolled past — every plant logged the day it's potted, and taken off the list the day it leaves with someone.

We don't stock what we can't grow well locally. If it's on the bench, someone here has kept it alive for at least a season first.
