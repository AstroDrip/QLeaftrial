# Sparkle Plante photo mapping

Written 2026-11-18 for stage 7. Source: the public product list of https://sparkleplante.qa/ (71 products, read
through its WooCommerce store list). QLeaves side: the live public catalogue (117 published products). Approval:
the owner confirmed on 2026-11-17 that Sparkle Plante approved QLeaves reusing its product photographs.

Method: products are matched by exact name only. For each exact match, only that product's photo was downloaded
and compared with the QLeaves photos using a perceptual fingerprint (0 = same picture), then looked at by eye to
confirm the plant. Nothing has been uploaded or changed on qleaves.qa; "Assigned" means "now on the QLeaves
product".

## Summary

- 23 exact matches already show this very Sparkle Plante photo on QLeaves (the first catalogue was imported from it).
- 4 exact matches (Aloe Vera, Spider Plant, Boston Fern, Chamaedorea Elegans) show the shop's own newer photos
  instead; the Sparkle Plante photo is the right plant and could be added as an extra photo if you want.
- 1 probable match by spelling (Philodendron Birkin) and 2 bougainvillea photos, see the table.
- 41 Sparkle Plante products have no QLeaves product of the same name; nothing is assigned for them.
- No photo is used for two products.

## Uncertain identifications: owner's decision

The photo report (`photo-report.md`) lists 39 products whose plant identification was uncertain. All 39 are
published on qleaves.qa (they were published together with the other drafts, at the demo price of 99 QAR).

Decision (owner, 2026-11-18): keep all 39 live. They are not unpublished; their names can be corrected in admin as
they are confirmed.
